Popkorner

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2, 2026

Popkorner ("we", "us", or "our") is a movie discovery app. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. Data We Collect

Data Type What Why
Account info Email address, display name Create and manage your account
Sign in with Apple Apple-provided identity token Authenticate without a password
Profile photo An image you optionally upload Personalize your profile
Reviews & ratings Your Pop/Top/Flop verdict, emotion reactions, and short text reviews Core app functionality
Watchlist Movies you mark as "to watch" or "watched" Track what you want to see
Cinema Weather location Your current device location, which may be precise or approximate depending on your iOS settings, after you grant permission Request a local forecast from Apple Weather and show Cinema Weather
AI feature input Mood and natural-language movie searches, questions you ask about a movie, and custom reaction text Return movie suggestions and answers, suggest an emoji for a custom reaction, and check custom reaction text for prohibited content
Device motion Accelerometer and gyroscope data (processed on-device only) Interactive 3D card effects; never transmitted to any server
Usage & diagnostics Which screens you open, actions you take (such as tapping a verdict or reaction), performance timings, crash and error reports, your IP address, and your approximate location (country and city) Understand how the app is used, fix bugs, improve performance, see which countries the app is used in, and investigate abuse or security problems

Cinema Weather asks iOS for your current location only when it needs a local forecast and you have granted location access while using the app. iOS may provide a precise or approximate location depending on your settings. The location is sent to Apple Weather to retrieve the forecast, cached temporarily in the app's memory, and not saved to Popkorner's database or used to create a location history. You can deny or change location access in iOS Settings; the rest of the app remains available.

Separately, our analytics provider works out an approximate country and city from your internet connection. This does not use your device's location services.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not use your current location for background tracking, advertising, or to create a location history
  • We do not collect your contacts or address book
  • We do not collect health, financial, or biometric data
  • We do not use advertising trackers or sell your data to advertisers
  • Note: Apple may also collect anonymized usage and crash data through its built-in App Analytics system, subject to your device privacy settings.

3. How We Collect Data

  • Directly from you — when you create an account, write a review, upload a photo, add a movie to your watchlist, enter a mood query, ask a movie question, or create a custom reaction.
  • Sign in with Apple — Apple provides an identity token and, if you choose, your email address.
  • Location services — after you grant permission, iOS provides a current location fix when Cinema Weather needs a local forecast.
  • Device sensors — motion data is read locally on your device for visual effects and is never sent to any server.
  • Automatically — our analytics and diagnostics tools collect usage, performance, crash, network, and device information when analytics is enabled.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following services to operate the app:

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — hosts our database, authentication, and file storage. Your account data, reviews, and watchlist are stored in Supabase's infrastructure. Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • TMDB (themoviedb.org) — provides movie metadata (titles, posters, descriptions, release dates). We query TMDB on your behalf; no personal data is sent to TMDB. TMDB Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI (openai.com) — processes text you submit through Mood Search, movie questions, and custom reactions, together with limited movie context needed to respond. We use OpenAI to return movie suggestions and answers, suggest custom reaction emojis, and moderate custom reaction labels. We do not send OpenAI your name or email address. OpenAI Privacy Policy and API Data Controls.
  • Apple Weather — receives your current location when Cinema Weather requests a local forecast. Apple states that the location used for a forecast is not associated with your identity. Apple Weather & Privacy.
  • Datadog (datadoghq.com) — provides analytics and crash and error reporting, so we can understand how the app is used, diagnose problems, and improve performance. We identify you to Datadog only by a random account identifier — never your email address or name. Datadog processes this data on our behalf in the United States. Datadog Privacy Policy.

We do not use any advertising or ad-targeting third-party services.

5. Links to Other Sites

The app may contain links to external websites (such as TMDB movie pages or streaming services). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies independently.

6. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the Popkorner app:

  • Authenticate you and maintain your session
  • Display your reviews, ratings, and watchlist
  • Show your profile to you (and, if you share a review card, to recipients)
  • Display your public profile, reviews, and activity to other Popkorner users who follow you or visit your profile
  • Provide local Cinema Weather forecasts when you grant location access
  • Process mood searches, answer movie questions, suggest custom reaction emojis, and moderate custom reaction labels
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making.

We process your current location with your consent when you enable Cinema Weather. You can withdraw that consent at any time in iOS Settings. We process AI feature input to provide the search, question, or reaction feature you request. Our legal basis for moderating custom reaction labels is our legitimate interest in keeping user-generated content safe.

Our legal basis for analytics and crash reporting is our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app. You can turn off analytics at any time in Settings, under Privacy.

7. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in Supabase's cloud infrastructure with the following protections:

  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS)
  • Database access is protected by Row Level Security — users can only access their own data
  • Authentication tokens are managed by Supabase Auth
  • Profile photos are stored in access-controlled Supabase Storage buckets

International Data Transfers

Datadog processes analytics and diagnostics data in the United States. OpenAI may process AI feature input outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where required, these transfers use recognized safeguards. Datadog is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and also relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. OpenAI's Data Processing Addendum uses adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers of European Economic Area data. OpenAI Data Processing Addendum.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we permanently and irreversibly delete the following from the app, immediately:

  • Your profile and account information
  • All reviews and ratings
  • Your watchlist
  • Your profile photo
  • Your authentication record

Popkorner does not store the text of your mood searches or movie questions. Our AI feature usage logs store only your random account identifier, the time of the request, and token usage for rate limiting and abuse prevention. A custom reaction label that you confirm is stored as part of your review. Deleting your account also deletes these usage logs and confirmed custom reactions from Popkorner.

OpenAI may retain API inputs and outputs as described in its API Data Controls. Apple handles location used for weather forecasts under Apple Weather & Privacy.

Analytics and diagnostics data held by Datadog is keyed to your random account identifier — not your email or name — and is retained for a limited period in line with Datadog’s retention policy. Deleting your account removes your data from the app immediately; you can also stop new analytics collection at any time from Settings.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data — view your profile, reviews, and watchlist directly in the app.
  • Delete your data — delete your account from the app (Settings > Account Settings > Delete Account). This permanently removes your data from the app.
  • Withdraw consent — you can stop using the app at any time. Deleting the app alone does not delete your account; use the in-app deletion option first.
  • Opt out of analytics — turn off usage analytics at any time in Settings, under Privacy.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you also have the right to request portability of your data and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In France, the relevant authority is the CNIL (cnil.fr). If you are unsatisfied with how we handle your data or a data request, please contact us first at privacy@popkorner.app so we can try to resolve it directly.

For data requests beyond what the app provides, contact us at privacy@popkorner.app.

10. Children's Privacy

Popkorner is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum digital age of consent in your country, if higher). We do not knowingly collect personal information from such children. If we learn we have collected data from a child under the applicable age minimum, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Effective date" above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
