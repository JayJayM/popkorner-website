About Popkorner

One line: Discover movies and series, track every release and new season, save your picks, see what your friends loved, and share your reviews beautifully.

Short: Popkorner is an iPhone app for people who love movies and series. Discover new trailers, what's new in theaters and streaming, and the latest series releases. Keep a living Library of everything you've watched and still want to watch, track how far you've watched in every series, and never miss a movie or new season you've been waiting for. Capture how each story made you feel and share beautiful reviews with friends.

Full: Popkorner is a movie-and-series discovery, tracking, and social app for iPhone, built for people who want more than a crowded catalog or a spreadsheet of star ratings. It keeps Movies and Series in two focused worlds while carrying you to the same place in either one. Discover new trailers, theatrical releases, new shows and seasons, streaming picks, collections, and moods from Spotlight. Build a living Library of what you've watched, what's next, and upcoming releases. For series, mark the last season you finished and let Popkorner surface the next available or announced season. Give every movie or series a verdict — Flop, Pop, or Top — add the emotion it left you with, and share a beautiful review card. Follow friends to see what they're watching and explore your combined taste and stats. Popkorner is made in France, with a lot of care for craft, motion, and the small details that make an app feel alive.
